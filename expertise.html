Main areas of expertise

Six capability areas. One accountable partner.

Each capability is offered as a standalone engagement or combined into a single program of work, delivered by the same senior partner from scoping through execution.

01 — Building + Executing

Operational Model

Designing a target operating model is only half the work. We build the governance and cadence that let it survive contact with the organisation.

  • Target operating model (TOM) design
  • Governance, decision rights, and cadences
  • Execution playbooks and KPI tracking
02 — Including Public → Private

Transformation

From privatization strategy to operational readiness, transformation work lives or dies on stakeholder alignment and disciplined risk governance.

  • Privatization strategy support and implementation
  • Operational audits and readiness assessments
  • Stakeholder alignment and risk governance
03 — Performance & Utilization

Efficiency Maximisation

Sustainable efficiency gains come from redesigning how work flows, not from a single round of headcount cuts.

  • Productivity baselines and performance levers
  • Facility, crew, and workflow optimisation
  • Cost structure improvements with controls
04 — Roles, Org, Controls

Restructuring

Restructuring is a governance exercise as much as an organisational one. We design both together so accountability survives the change.

  • Org redesign and role clarity
  • Workforce and process restructuring
  • Governance, reporting, and accountability

Infrastructure Build

We stay engaged from requirements definition through vendor negotiation to final acceptance — the stages where infrastructure programs typically lose control.

  • Requirements, architecture, capacity planning
  • RFP packs, evaluation, negotiation support
  • Delivery oversight, acceptance, and handover

International Formats
