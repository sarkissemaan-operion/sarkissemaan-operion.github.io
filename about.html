Who we are

A boutique built to close the gap between strategy and execution.

OPERION Advisory is a senior-led practice founded to help executives execute complex change with clarity, discretion, and control — without the layers, junior staffing, or one-size-fits-all frameworks of a large firm.

SS

Sarkis Semaan

Founder & Managing Partner

Sarkis is a media executive with more than 20 years of leadership experience driving transformation, privatization, and operational restructuring. He has led high-impact consultancy engagements for national broadcasters and media entities — including privatization strategy, infrastructure due diligence, and large-scale operational audits.

He founded OPERION on a simple premise: the work that matters most to an organisation is rarely the strategy document. It is what happens after it is approved — the operating model that gets built, the transformation that gets delivered, the infrastructure that gets commissioned on schedule and on budget.

That premise shapes how every engagement is staffed and run. There is no bench of junior consultants learning on the client's time. There is a senior partner, a tight scope, and a commitment to outputs that stand up to board and government-level scrutiny.

How we work

Principles that govern every engagement, not just the pitch.

Principle 01

Discretion first

Confidential by default. NDA-ready. Engagements involving sensitive government or board matters are handled with the same rigor as public ones — and disclosed only with permission.

Principle 02

Senior-led, always

The partner who scopes the engagement delivers it. No handoffs, no rotating staff, no learning curve at the client's expense.

Principle 03

Outcomes over decks

Every deliverable is built to be acted on: decision logs, blueprints, RFP packs, and governance structures — not slideware.
