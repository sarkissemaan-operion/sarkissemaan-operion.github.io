How an engagement runs

Four stages. No surprises.

Diagnose

A short, confidential intake to baseline the current state, constraints, and what "done" looks like.

Design

The operating model, roadmap, or RFP scope is drafted and pressure-tested with your stakeholders.

Deliver

Execution with a fixed cadence, a decision log, and a single accountable partner running point.

Sustain

Handover of governance, KPIs, and playbooks so outcomes hold after the engagement ends.
